# Round-one demo photo credits

All photos below are genuine public road-maintenance photographs. The app ships downscaled Wikimedia Commons thumbnail copies (maximum edge 1280 px) for an offline presentation. Each source link includes the original description, photographer and license. The patched-road and asphalt-pothole copies are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0; the repair-work image is a U.S. federal public-domain work.

The outcomes and explanations shown in VERIDEX are fixed demo copy, not dataset labels or model predictions. Photos come from different dates and places and are **not** matched before/after pairs. Do not treat this three-photo presentation pack as an accuracy benchmark or training set.
